## 6. Precision of the summary figures

Bootstrap over auction sales: 5000 resamples (seed 20261104); each draws whole sales (house and day, all their lots together), with replacement, as many as observed, and computes the measure again. (A) the measured makers kept: the uncertainty given the qualifying makers. (B) the whole selection rerun in each resample (deceased makers, 10 sales over 15 years checked again; regional medians from the makers measured in that resample). Spread = standard deviation across resamples, with the 2.5–97.5 % range. For the three groups the range is shifted (resampling adds noise to each rate and moves makers into the outer groups): read their standard deviation.

## 7. Matching: the chance test

The matching is run again with the sale dates of one group of sources moved forward by one year: no real lot can then find its partner, so every pair still accepted is a coincidence. Chance pairs divided by the pairs the real run accepts between the same two sources estimate the share of accepted matches that are false.

- All pairs of sources tested: **0.46 %** of accepted matches are estimated false.
- The largest pair of sources: 0.55 %; the highest rate among the pairs with at least 1 000 accepted matches: 1.03 %.
